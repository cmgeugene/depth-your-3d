# Control-signal representations for carrying motion + camera from live action into a generative video model while dropping performer shape/identity (state as of 2026-11-15)

Evidence tags used below: **[Paper]** = arXiv / peer-reviewed; **[Vendor]** = official product documentation; **[Practitioner]** = GitHub READMEs, community workflows, issue threads, third-party guides (anecdotal, usually no controlled test). Dates are publication/measurement dates of the source, not of the underlying model.

---

## Q1. For each representation: what survives, what leaks, known artefacts

### Takeaway
Every representation that encodes the performer's *surface or outline* (edges > SMPL mesh / DensePose / normals > depth) leaks silhouette and proportions; depth is the least bad of that group and is the only single-pass signal that also carries camera, props and scene layout. Sparse skeletons are the standard "shape-light" body signal, but (a) on a black background they carry no camera/scene, and (b) 2025–2026 papers agree that even 2D skeletons still leak the driver's limb proportions unless they are retargeted or bone-length-augmented.

### Cited Findings

#### Summary matrix (compiled from the findings cited below; cells without a citation below are in "Inferences")

| Representation | Body pose | Hands | Face/expression | Camera | Scene layout / props | Leaks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2D skeleton (OpenPose/DWPose/ViTPose) on black | yes, but front/back + occlusion ambiguity | only if hand keypoints rendered | landmarks = coarse, "limited in fine-grained facial expression" | **no** | **no** | driver's bone proportions / relative size |
| 3D skeleton rendered as cylinders (SCAIL) | yes, with occlusion order | via overlaid 2D hand keypoints | via overlaid 2D face keypoints | no (camera only used for retargeting fit) | no | low; designed "identity-agnostic" |
| SMPL / SMPL-X mesh render, DensePose, normal/semantic maps from SMPL | strong 3D prior | weak ("limited") | weak | no | no | **body shape** ("severe identity leakage") unless β swapped to target |
| Depth video (monocular, per-frame or video-depth) | yes | coarse | effectively none ("carries no face") | **yes** (parallax) | **yes** | **silhouette, proportions, bare-body shape** |
| Edges (Canny / HED / lineart) | yes | yes | yes | yes | yes | outline + texture/clothing detail (most) |
| Optical flow / point tracks / warped noise | yes (dense or sparse) | sparse | no | yes (global flow) | partially | low shape, but needs a model trained/adapted for it |
| Camera-only (Plücker, pose, point-cloud re-render) | no | no | no | **yes** | first-frame geometry | none of the performer |
| Degraded RGB (blur/downscale/gray) | yes | coarse | coarse | yes | yes | graded: identity/shape falls with degradation, so does detail |

#### 2D skeleton / keypoint renders
- **[Paper, Sep 2025]** Wan-Animate uses a ViTPose 2D skeleton for the body *because* it "better caters to the majority of mainstream use cases", generalises better to non-humanoid characters, and because SMPL renderings "contain the character's shape information", which can make the model rely on shape cues. Face is driven by raw face crops encoded to implicit latents (not landmarks); the authors say landmarks lose fine detail and demand precise retargeting. — [Wan-Animate, arXiv 2509.14055](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14055)
- **[Paper, Sep 2025]** In Wan-Animate "Animation Mode" the background is taken from the *source image* ("the background from the source image is preserved"); the paper claims generated actions "that include camera movements" but describes no camera-control mechanism. — [Wan-Animate](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14055)
- **[Paper, Dec 2025]** One-to-All Animation lists as an author-stated limitation that pose-only conditioning gives limited control over camera trajectories. — [One-to-All Animation, arXiv 2511.22940](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22940v2)
- **[Paper, Dec 2025; v3 Mar 2026]** SCAIL: 2D skeletons (DWPose, ViTPose) "suffer from noisy predictions and cannot encode occlusion"; "the inherent ambiguity of 2D pose can result in incorrect pose interpretations" (front/back limb confusion). Replacing the 3D-cylinder skeleton with 2D pose in the same model worsens self-driven FVD from 228.62 to 295.36 and PSNR 18.08 → 17.08 (SCAIL-1.3B, Table 2). — [SCAIL, arXiv 2512.05905](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05905v3)
- **[Paper, Jun 2026, ECCV 2026]** EMOSH: mainstream 2D-pose approaches "suffer from severe 'Motion-Shape Entanglement'" — 2D skeletons implicitly encode the driver's body proportions, so the driver's physique leaks into the output, even with pose retargeting. — [EMOSH, arXiv 2606.28026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28026); [EMOSH project page](https://eastbeanzhang.github.io/EMOSH/)
- **[Paper, Jan 2026]** DreamActor-M2 (ByteDance): pose-aligned channel-wise injection methods "frequently suffer from 'shape leakage', whereby the structural priors embedded in the driving signal distort the reference identity." — [DreamActor-M2, arXiv 2601.21716](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21716v1)
- **[Paper, Dec 2025]** One-to-All: "the generated face identity is influenced by both reference appearance and driving pose skeleton" (i.e. face keypoints leak face geometry); their fix randomly alters facial skeletons in 70% of training samples. — [One-to-All Animation](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22940v2)
- **[Paper, Dec 2025]** SCAIL still relies on facial landmarks for face control, which is "inherently limited in fine-grained facial expression." — [SCAIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05905v3)
- **[Practitioner, 2026]** ComfyUI-ReShot ships pose (DWPose) with hands on and **face off by default**, "because the face shape is what ReShot throws away"; it describes pose as carrying "only the people, every limb exactly, nothing of their shape or the set." — [ComfyUI-ReShot README](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)
- **[Practitioner, Nov 2025]** In the Kijai WanVideoWrapper issue thread, a user suggests that for cartoon targets removing the face skeleton "and just leave body and hand" may look more natural. — [kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper #1262](https://github.com/kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper/issues/1262)

#### Dense body representations (SMPL/SMPL-X renders, DensePose, semantic part maps)
- **[Paper, Mar 2024, ECCV 2024]** Champ renders the SMPL sequence into depth, normal and semantic maps and adds a DWPose skeleton for faces/fingers. Per the paper: depth mainly gives body contours, normal gives body orientation, semantic gives body-part distinctions, skeleton gives face/hand detail. Ablation (TikTok, FVD↓): skeleton only (w/o SMPL) 192.34; w/o depth+normal 189.07; w/o skeleton 184.24; full 170.20. Limitation: SMPL "modeling capacity for faces and hands is limited." — [Champ, arXiv 2403.14781](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14781)
- **[Paper, Mar 2024]** Champ's cross-identity recipe is *parametric shape alignment*: the reference character's SMPL shape β_ref is substituted into every driving pose before rendering, so the rendered guidance already has the target's body shape. — [Champ](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14781)
- **[Paper, May 2024]** VividPose makes the same argument from the other side: 2D-estimator pose conditions cannot be modified to match the reference's body shape, "this misalignment results in inaccurate human body shape generation"; it fits SMPL-X to both reference and driver to fix it. — [VividPose, arXiv 2405.18156](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.18156v1)
- **[Paper, Dec 2025]** SCAIL: SMPL mesh controls "offer strong 3D human priors but cause severe identity leakage", and are "inherently human-specific and offer limited controllability for augmentation or retargeting." — [SCAIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05905v3)
- **[Paper, Feb 2025, ICCV 2025]** Animate Anyone 2 rejected dense SMPL meshes because they "compromise the generalizability across characters." — [Animate Anyone 2, arXiv 2502.06145](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06145v1)
- **[Paper, Jun 2026]** EMOSH's summary of the trade-off: 3D-prior methods such as SMPL avoid the 2D-skeleton shape leak (when shape and pose parameters are separated) but struggle with facial expressions and complex gestures, giving stiff results; it moves to an Expressive Human Model with explicit shape/pose disentanglement. — [EMOSH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28026)
- **[Paper, May 2025]** MTVCrafter argues 2D-rendered pose images fail when the driving pose images "significantly deviate from the reference appearance in shape or position" and tokenises raw 4D (3D-over-time) motion instead. — [MTVCrafter, arXiv 2505.10238](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.10238v4)

#### Depth maps, normals, depth+pose
- **[Paper, Feb 2023]** Runway Gen-1 chose monocular depth (MiDaS) as the structure signal because depth estimates "encode significantly less content information" than alternatives, while edge filters "also detect textures in a video which limits the range of artistic control over content." It explicitly states the limitation the studio is hitting: "Depth maps reveal the silhouttes of objects which prevents content edits involving large changes in object shape." — [Gen-1, arXiv 2302.03011](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2302.03011)
- **[Paper, Feb 2023]** Gen-1 also says "A perfect separation of content and structure is difficult" and "a fundamental overlap between content and structure information remains." — [Gen-1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2302.03011)
- **[Paper, Feb 2025]** Animate Anyone 2 treats raw depth as a shape-leak risk: its "depth-wise pose modulation" uses depth **only inside the skeleton region** (depth masked by the skeleton) to add inter-limb spatial ordering without the outline; adds Conv3D temporal smoothing for noisy estimates. Ablation without pose modulation vs full: FVD 211.15 vs 186.17, LPIPS 0.301 vs 0.276. — [Animate Anyone 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06145v1)
- **[Practitioner, 2026]** Comfy hub "Depth Motion Capture with Seedance 2.0" (author Purz; node is labelled "Seedance 2.5 Reference to Video (Legacy)"): Depth Anything V2 per-frame depth video → Seedance `video_1`, look from `image_1`; "The depth map dictates how things move; the image dictates what they are." Claims preserved: camera moves, body motion, scene geometry (parallax, relative distances). Stated weaknesses: depth quality drops with motion blur/low light → flicker/distortion on fast motion. No measured comparison with pose. — [Comfy hub workflow](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/depth-motion-capture-with-seedance-2-0-085a4bba12f8/)
- **[Practitioner, Jul–Nov 2026]** OSideMedia depth-converter (Video Depth Anything Small → Seedance): depth "keeps what pose skeletons throw away" (camera path, props, scene geometry), "strips appearance", "carries no face". Author's adherence test (2026-07-25): generated videos re-converted to depth correlate with the reference's motion profile at r = +0.950 / +0.916 / +0.926 at zero lag ("Timing transferred beat for beat"). Notes that depth removes clothing so figures read as bare body shapes; one kit with two prone overlapping silhouettes filling the frame was refused at input. Motion parallel to the image plane survives poorly in depth (a prone crawl kept 16% of its RGB motion energy vs 87% for a sword duel). — [OSideMedia/depth-converter README](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **[Practitioner, 2026]** ReShot: depth keeps "where people stand, how big they are, how they move and how the camera moves" and drops "faces, wardrobe, lighting and style" — i.e. size/placement is explicitly part of what depth transfers. — [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)
- **[Practitioner]** Wan2GP VACE docs: with Depth as the control process the regenerated background "can keep geometry closer to the original control video". RunComfy's Wan 2.2 VACE page claims depth guidance helps "retain scene layout, hand pose, and occlusion" (vendor-tutorial claim, no test). — [Wan2GP VACE.md](https://github.com/deepbeepmeep/Wan2GP/blob/main/docs/VACE.md); [RunComfy Wan 2.2 VACE](https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-workflows/wan-2-2-vace-in-comfyui-pose-driven-motion-video-workflow)

#### Edges (Canny / HED / lineart)
- **[Paper, Feb 2023]** Gen-1: edge filters "also detect textures", limiting content edits (see above) — the paper-level reason edges are the leakiest structural signal. — [Gen-1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2302.03011)
- **[Paper, Jul 2025]** MotionShot observes that structure-conditioned editors "VideoComposer, Gen-1, and Control-A-Video are constrained by the structure of the original video", and that landmark, depth/edge and trajectory cues "often struggle with objects that have substantial appearance discrepancies." — [MotionShot, arXiv 2507.16310](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.16310v1)
- **[Practitioner]** Comfy's VACE control-video template: different preprocessors "change how strictly structure is preserved"; relaxing Canny thresholds makes edges "less constraining". — [Comfy hub: Wan2.1 VACE Control Video](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/wan2-1-vace-control-video-2652985596d8/)
- **[Practitioner]** A Blender→ComfyUI VACE workflow's rule of thumb: "depth when you need spatial fidelity, canny for clean composition control, and pose for character‑focused motion." — [RunComfy Blender-to-ComfyUI renderer](https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-workflows/blender-to-comfyui-ai-renderer-2-0-workflow-cinematic-video-output)

#### Motion-only signals (flow, point tracks, warped noise) and camera-only signals
- **[Paper, Mar 2025]** VACE accepts any control "represented and stored as RGB videos", listing "depth, grayscale, pose, scribble, optical flow, and layout"; all go through the same video VAE into the latent space. The paper does not compare control types against each other. — [VACE, arXiv 2503.07598](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.07598)
- **[Paper, Jan 2025, CVPR 2025]** Go-with-the-Flow replaces i.i.d. initial noise with noise warped along optical flow; covers object motion, camera motion via global flow fields, and motion transfer from an input video. It requires fine-tuning a video model on warped noise (data-only change). — [Go-with-the-Flow, arXiv 2501.08331](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.08331v2)
- **[Paper, Nov 2025]** Time-to-Move (training-free; demonstrated on SVD, CogVideoX, Wan 2.2 14B) uses *crude reference animations* ("cut-and-drag or depth-based reprojection") as an SDEdit-style motion signal. It documents the single-strength trade-off: at low noise "the denoised video adheres closely to the warped signal but inherits artifacts such as frozen backgrounds"; at high noise it drifts from the motion. "Dual-clock" denoising gives masked (motion-specified) regions strong adherence and the rest weak adherence. Reported: CoTracker distance 13.665 vs 27.844 for Go-with-the-Flow on CogVideoX; camera MSE 0.022 vs 0.033 on DL3DV. It describes Motion Prompting as a track-conditioned ControlNet on point tracks that requires extensive fine-tuning of the base model. — [Time-to-Move, arXiv 2511.08633](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08633v1)
- **[Paper, Apr 2025]** Uni3C unifies camera + human motion: camera via a point cloud lifted from first-frame monocular depth and re-rendered along the trajectory (PCDController); human motion via SMPL-X aligned into the same world space. — [Uni3C, arXiv 2504.14899](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14899)
- **[Paper, 2025]** ReCamMaster (ICCV 2025) re-renders an input video along new camera trajectories by video conditioning; TrajectoryCrafter instead lifts the source to a point cloud via monocular depth and uses renders as control; CamCloneMaster does reference-based camera cloning. Competing-paper comparisons note TrajectoryCrafter "often suffers from geometric errors and visual artifacts" and that ReCamMaster keeps the first frame identical to the source. — [ReCamMaster, arXiv 2503.11647](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.11647v2); [CamCloneMaster, arXiv 2506.03140](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.03140); [CameraAnything, arXiv 2607.24591](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.24591)

#### Degraded video (blur / downscale / low-frequency) — the structure-vs-content knob
- **[Paper, Feb 2023]** Gen-1's structure-fidelity knob: "We then apply t_s iterations of blurring and downsampling to the depth maps", "where t_s controls the amount of structure to preserve from the input video"; blur is "an information-destroying process ... which improves stability compared to other approaches such as adding noise". The model is *trained* with t_s sampled 0–7 and receives t_s as an input embedding; "At inference, this parameter can be controlled to achieve different editing effects." — [Gen-1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2302.03011)
- **[Vendor, Jun 2025]** Luma Modify Video (Ray2) productises the same knob as nine presets — adhere / flex / reimagine × 1–3 — and says it uses "performance signals like pose, facial expression, and scene structure" to decide what to preserve. — [Luma: Introducing Modify Video](https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-modify-video); [Luma Modify Video API docs](https://docs.lumalabs.ai/docs/modify-video)
- **[Paper, Jan 2026]** DreamActor-M2's end-to-end variant feeds the **raw RGB driving video** as in-context input (no pose extraction) and learns "to transfer motion patterns directly from raw RGB sequences"; it outperforms the pose variant on cases ambiguous in 2D keypoints (direction ambiguity, overlapping hands). This is a trained LoRA on Seedance 1.0, not zero-shot behaviour. — [DreamActor-M2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21716v1)
- **[Practitioner, Sep 2026]** ReShot, for MiniMax H3: set depth `max_side` to 320 — "A small one carries the moves without the shape"; failure mode "Face drifts toward the reference (H3): the depth video is too large. Lower max_side." Also measured that a faster/coarser depth model changes edge energy only −4.5% while "large shapes, positions and motion are identical". — [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)

#### Training-free / reference-based motion transfer designed around leakage
- **[Paper, Dec 2024, CVPR 2025]** DiTFlow (training-free, DiT) extracts patch-wise "attention motion flow" from the reference and guides latents; authors report first/second on all metrics vs SMM, MOFT, MotionClone on 50 DAVIS videos, and characterise MotionClone as UNet attention guidance with "less fine-grained disentanglement of motion". — [DiTFlow, arXiv 2412.07776](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.07776v2)
- **[Paper, Jul 2025]** MotionShot (training-free): attention-based methods "perform well with objects that share similar appearances and structures but often struggle with distinct objects" because of "strong coupling between motion and structure"; its fix is semantic feature matching plus **reference-to-target shape retargeting** — a thin-plate-spline warp that reshapes the reference object in the reference frames into the target's shape before motion extraction. User-study motion preservation 4.95 vs 2.80–3.00 for VideoComposer/Gen-1/VMC/MotionClone (20 volunteers, authors' own study). Limitation: works "mostly when reference-target objects share similar semantic." — [MotionShot](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.16310v1)
- **[Paper, BMVC 2025]** "Motion by Queries" reports that video generation needs more query-injection steps, "causing identity leakage from the unconstrained source video", and that cross-class transfer separates identity better than same-class transfer. — [Motion by Queries, BMVC 2025](https://bmva-archive.org.uk/bmvc/2025/assets/papers/Paper_159/paper.pdf)
- **[Paper, 2026]** Later papers describe MotionDirector-style methods as fine-tuning the backbone per reference video, and DiTFlow as zero-shot/inversion-free but extracting noisy motion signals. — [FastVMT, arXiv 2602.05551](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.05551v3); [MotionGrounder, arXiv 2604.00853](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.00853v1)

### Inferences
- The studio's observation (depth = clay model, no face leak, but silhouette lock-in) is exactly the limitation stated in the Gen-1 paper in 2023 and the reason Animate Anyone 2 refuses to give the model depth outside the skeleton region. It is a property of the representation, not a Seedance quirk.
- Leakage ordering implied by the papers (most → least performer shape): edges/lineart > SMPL mesh ≈ DensePose ≈ normal map > depth > 2D skeleton (un-retargeted) > retargeted / bone-augmented skeleton ≈ 3D cylinder skeleton > point tracks / flow > camera-only. Normal maps and DensePose were not separately ablated for leakage in any source found; they are placed with SMPL renders because they encode the same body surface.
- "Blurring depth loses camera and detail" is the expected behaviour of a Gen-1-style knob applied to a model that was *not* trained with a blur-level embedding: Gen-1 tells the network how blurred the input is; a zero-shot model just sees a mushy video.
- All attention/latent-guidance methods (DiTFlow, MotionClone, MotionShot, MotionDirector, Time-to-Move) need access to the model's latents, attention or weights. They cannot be applied to a closed API such as Seedance; only their *pre-processing ideas* transfer (e.g. MotionShot's "warp the reference into the target's shape first").

### Gaps
- No paper or practitioner test was found that measures *shape leakage* across representations with a single fixed model (e.g. same clip as depth vs normal vs DensePose vs skeleton). All ordering above is assembled from separate papers' qualitative statements.
- No source found on posterisation/quantised depth, grayscale-only RGB, or optical-flow colour-wheel videos as *zero-shot* references for a general-purpose model.
- Surface-normal video as a driving signal: only covered indirectly via Champ (normals rendered from SMPL). No evidence about normals from monocular estimators leaking more or less than depth.
- Sapiens pose specifically: no source found comparing it against DWPose/ViTPose for this purpose.
- How much facial expression survives a depth pass was not measured anywhere; the only statements are "carries no face" (practitioner) and that landmarks are coarse (papers).
- Motion Prompting, Tora, DragAnything, MotionCtrl, CameraCtrl (Plücker), MotionClone, MotionDirector, Motion Inversion, SMM/Space-Time Diffusion Features, VMC and MotionShop were not individually opened; they appear here only through descriptions in other papers.

## Q2. Robustness when the target has very different proportions (chibi, big-head anime, non-human); what the literature recommends

### Takeaway
The consensus of 2024–2026 character-animation work is that a driving signal must be **re-proportioned to the target before it reaches the generator** (bone-length retargeting, shape-parameter swap, or shape-randomising augmentation) and that anything pixel-aligned to the performer's outline (masks, depth, mesh) makes the model "forcibly conform". Practitioners confirm that un-retargeted skeletons make Wan Animate stretch chibi characters to the actor's skeleton.

### Cited Findings
- **[Paper, Sep 2025]** Wan-Animate animation mode retargets the driving skeleton: per-limb length ratios between the two characters are used to rescale the driver's bones, then the pose is translated to the character's position (anchor = feet for full-body, neck for half-body/portrait). Because 2D bone lengths depend on posture, they optionally edit both reference and driver's first frame into a T-pose with an image-editing model (Qwen-Image, FLUX.1 Kontext cited) and compute scale factors there. In replacement mode retargeting is discouraged to preserve environment interaction, and "may result in some deformation" when shapes differ a lot. — [Wan-Animate](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14055)
- **[Paper, Oct 2024, ICLR 2025]** Animate-X (anthropomorphic characters): existing methods impose the pose "rigidly onto the target character"; self-driven training "aligns reference and pose skeletons by body shape", hiding the mismatch until inference. Its Explicit Pose Indicator realigns each driving pose to a random anchor pose's body shape and rescales body, legs, arms, neck, shoulders and face size (and adds/drops parts) with probability λ > 98%. Ablation on A2Bench (FVD↓): full 703.87; w/o EPI 948.25; w/o realign 860.25; w/o rescale 721.11; w/o neck/face rescale 878.11; w/o body/shoulder rescale 850.17. — [Animate-X, arXiv 2410.10306](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10306v2)
- **[Paper, Jan 2026]** DreamActor-M2 (ByteDance, Seedance 1.0 backbone) fights shape leakage with **random bone-length scaling (30% of samples) and bounding-box normalisation** of the 2D skeleton, "decoupling body shape from motion semantics", and feeds the motion as spatiotemporal in-context tokens rather than pose-aligned channels. Because augmentation blurs fine cues (e.g. clasped "prayer" hands), an MLLM writes a motion-describing text prompt to compensate. Ablation (human rating, motion / appearance): full 4.18 / 4.12; w/o pose augmentation 3.80 / 3.92; w/o text guidance 3.85 / 4.08. Benchmark has 40 human→cartoon pairs; non-human results (rabbit, bottle, Pikachu, Detective Conan) are qualitative only. — [DreamActor-M2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21716v1)
- **[Paper, Dec 2025]** SCAIL: "2D skeleton rescaling ... inherently suffers from deformation due to inconsistency with 3D motion dynamics." It instead augments in 3D (random bone-scale sets, rate 0.8) and at inference leaves the 3D skeleton untouched and "only optimize[s] the camera" to fit the reference. Benchmark includes ~40 non-real characters, incl. "thin-limb anime characters" and a very short plush toy. — [SCAIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05905v3)
- **[Paper, Dec 2025]** One-to-All: methods relying on retargeting are fragile — "its failure directly causes identity drift"; under misaligned inputs MimicMotion and StableAnimator "completely lose appearance information", UniAnimate and Wan-Animate "show degraded quality". On their Cartoon set One-to-All-14B FVD 403.47 vs Wan-Animate 485.92 vs UniAnimate-DiT 510.61. User study vs Wan-Animate on 30 misaligned cases (4 participants): 69.2% vs 19.2% for seen-region fidelity. — [One-to-All Animation](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22940v2)
- **[Paper, Feb 2025]** Animate Anyone 2's "shape-agnostic mask": the character mask is split into patches and each patch set to its max (k_h = h/10, k_w = w/10 at inference), plus random scale augmentation; the "core idea" is "disrupting the correspondence between mask region and character outline." Precise masks are "suboptimal, potentially introducing shape leakage", and when target differs from source "the model may forcibly conform to the mask boundary." Still, "deformation artifacts may emerge when source and target characters exhibit substantial shape discrepancies." — [Animate Anyone 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06145v1)
- **[Paper, Mar 2024 / May 2024 / Jun 2026]** Parametric-model route: Champ swaps the reference's SMPL β into the driving poses; VividPose does the same with SMPL-X; EMOSH uses an Expressive Human Model with explicit shape/pose separation and says 2D-pose methods leak physique "even with pose retargeting". — [Champ](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14781); [VividPose](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.18156v1); [EMOSH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28026)
- **[Paper, Feb 2026]** IM-Animation: cross-reenactment between very different builds (child ↔ tall adult) "poses substantial difficulty due to drastic mismatches in body shape and spatial layout"; uses an implicit motion representation with mask-token retargeting. — [IM-Animation, arXiv 2602.07498](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.07498v1)
- **[Practitioner, Sep 2025]** Kijai WanVideoWrapper issue #1262 (user snicolast, 2025-09-19): "You can't accurately transfer an actor's performance onto a cartoon character with very different proportions (like a chibi) ... The system just ends up trying to stretch the character to match the skeleton." Workarounds offered (RuneGjerde): switch off skeleton guidance for body/face/hands (motion becomes "less an exact clone of the driving video"), or generate the cartoon character from the driving video's first frame "so that there is no need for the model to stretch anything" — which the original poster notes "isn't viable when you're dealing with already-established characters." — [kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper #1262](https://github.com/kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper/issues/1262)
- **[Practitioner, 2025–2026]** Community tooling for this exists: grmchn's ComfyUI-ProportionChanger (ported from the WanVideo UniAnimate pose detector) edits skeleton proportions and includes a "Mascot Pose Detector" for non-human-shaped characters; a request to expose Wan-Animate's official pose retargeting in ComfyUI is tracked in issue #1271. — [ComfyUI-ProportionChanger](https://github.com/grmchn/ComfyUI-ProportionChanger); [kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper #1271](https://github.com/kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper/issues/1271)
- **[Vendor, 2025–2026]** Kling Motion Control user guide: recommends real-human motion references; "certain stylized humanoid or humanoid body proportions can be recognized"; advises that the character image's proportions and full-/half-body framing "match those in the video for optimal results." — [Kling Motion Control user guide](https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide)
- **[Practitioner, 2026]** ReShot's prompt-level handling of scale with a depth reference (MiniMax H3): "Change the species, keep the size ratio" — demo prompt constrained the replacement to "about 1.3× the rabbit, never more than 1.5×." — [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)

### Inferences
- For a chibi / big-head target the literature points to three workable strategies, in decreasing order of evidence: (1) retarget the skeleton to the character's bone lengths (Wan-Animate/UniAnimate-style, ideally measured on a T-pose of the character); (2) do the retargeting in 3D and re-render (SCAIL, Champ, EMOSH) to avoid 2D foreshortening errors; (3) deliberately de-specify shape (bbox normalisation, random bone scaling, blocky masks) and let text + the anchor image fill in — but (3) is a *training-time* technique in every paper; its zero-shot effect on a general model is unverified.
- Head-size mismatch is the weakest point for skeleton signals: Animate-X's ablation shows neck/face rescaling matters as much as body rescaling, and practitioners drop face keypoints entirely for cartoon targets. For a big-head design, omit face landmarks and represent the head with a single retargeted proxy (sphere/keypoint) rather than the actor's facial outline.
- Retargeting breaks contact with the plate: Wan-Animate disables it in replacement mode precisely to keep environment interaction. Any hybrid where a rescaled character signal is composited over un-rescaled scene depth will have foot-contact / prop-contact mismatches that the model must paper over; anchoring at the feet (Wan-Animate's full-body anchor) minimises ground-contact error at the cost of head/hand position error.
- A depth map is, in these papers' vocabulary, a "precise mask plus relief" of the performer — the class of signal Animate Anyone 2 says makes the model "forcibly conform". No amount of prompt wording is likely to fully override it, consistent with the OSideMedia note that "the ref outranks the prompt".

### Gaps
- No paper evaluates chibi (2–3 heads tall) targets quantitatively; "cartoon" test sets are small (One-to-All: 12 pairs; DreamActor-M2: 40 pairs; SCAIL: ~40 non-real characters) and mostly near-human proportions.
- UniAnimate / UniAnimate-DiT and MimicMotion pose-alignment details were not opened directly; they are represented here via One-to-All's comparison and the community port (ProportionChanger).
- No evidence on whether Seedance 2.x / Kling O1 internally retarget a skeleton reference to the anchor image's proportions.

## Q3. Which representations do general-purpose "omni-reference" models understand zero-shot as a plain reference video?

### Takeaway
For Seedance 2.0/2.5 there is practitioner evidence (not official documentation) that a grayscale **depth video** is read as a motion + camera reference; official docs only say a reference video can be cited for "motion and camera movement" and expose **no reference-strength parameter**. Skeleton, Canny and other control-map inputs to Seedance are shipped in community tools but untested by their own authors; dedicated control stacks (Wan VACE / Wan-Animate, Kling Motion Control, Luma Modify, Runway Act-Two) are the only places where support for specific signals is documented.

### Cited Findings
- **[Vendor, 2026]** BytePlus ModelArk Seedance 2.5 docs: omni-reference accepts up to 10 reference videos (5 for 2.5 flash, 3 for the 2.0 series), 30 images, 10 audio; total reference audio/video duration raised from 15 s to 30 s; role `reference_video`; prompts cite assets by label, e.g. "referring to the composition of @Video1" and "referring to the motion and camera movement of @Video2". No strength/weight parameter is described in the portion of the page read (first ~100k characters of ~185k). — [BytePlus ModelArk: Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- **[Practitioner, Jun 2026]** Third-party explainer: in omni-reference "Reference videos here guide motion and atmosphere — they don't get animated directly"; first/last-frame slots take images only and (per this source) cannot be mixed with omni-reference inputs in the same generation; identifiable real faces and copyrighted visuals in the reference are blocked by a visual detection layer. — [clipdance.ai, 2026-06-16](https://clipdance.ai/blog/seedance-2-reference-video-rejected)
- **[Practitioner, 2026]** Depth → Seedance works in published community workflows: Comfy hub template (Purz) and OSideMedia depth-converter (r ≈ 0.92–0.95 motion-profile correlation, 2026-07-25). The latter's summary: Seedance treats a video reference as a "motion + camera channel", and "The ref outranks the prompt" (with one identity prompted against a two-body reference, the model cloned that identity onto both bodies). — [Comfy hub workflow](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/depth-motion-capture-with-seedance-2-0-085a4bba12f8/); [OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **[Practitioner, Jul–Nov 2026]** Resolution sensitivity on Seedance 2.0 (author's measurements): motion-ratio error +8% at 720p, +22% to +32% at 1080p (0 of 3 adhered; props dissolved, camera movement invented), +6% at 4K — "test at 480p, confirm at 720p, master at 720p or 4K. Never 1080p." The author says this does not carry over to 2.5, whose 1080p run adhered in two tests (2026-08-24). References beyond ~10 s and multi-signal clips degrade; sweet spot 3–8 s with one clear motion. — [OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **[Practitioner, 2026]** OSideMedia lists experimental reference variants — `contrast` (2–98% stretch), `invert`, `midgrey` (luma capped at 190), `edges` (Canny on depth) — and states none are validated. ReShot emits depth, DWPose and Canny in Seedance's input spec (24 fps, H.264, ≥ 407,696 pixels) but states of the Seedance path "We have not run this node ourselves"; its advice on depth vs pose is "Make both when unsure and see which the model follows better." — [OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter); [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)
- **[Practitioner, 2026]** Other general models: ReShot's main tested target is MiniMax H3, which follows a grey depth clip but needs the prompt to narrate the clip and to say the grey look is not to be copied (otherwise a "grey film" is returned). — [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)
- **[Paper, Jan 2026]** ByteDance's DreamActor-M2 shows the Seedance 1.0 backbone can be LoRA-adapted to take either an augmented 2D skeleton video or a raw RGB driving video as *in-context* motion input (concatenated spatiotemporally with the reference image), and reports GSB preference over Kling 2.6 (+9.66%), Kling-O1 (+43.66%) and Wan2.2-Animate (+51.43%). — [DreamActor-M2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21716v1)
- **[Paper, Mar 2025] / [Practitioner]** Wan VACE is *trained* on depth, grayscale, pose, scribble, optical flow and layout control videos; Wan 2.2 Fun/VACE control weights are described as supporting Pose, Depth, MLSD, Canny and trajectory control; VACE nodes expose strength and start/end percent. — [VACE](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.07598); [RunComfy Wan 2.2 VACE](https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-workflows/wan-2-2-vace-in-comfyui-pose-driven-motion-video-workflow)
- **[Vendor]** Kling Motion Control (2.6/3.0) takes a *live-action* single-character motion video (3–30 s, one continuous shot, whole body and head visible) plus a character image. "Character Orientation Matches Video" makes movements, expressions, **camera movement** and orientation follow the reference; "Matches Image" keeps motion/expressions but lets camera be prompted. The guide also advises avoiding cuts and camera movements in the motion reference. — [Kling Motion Control user guide](https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide)
- **[Vendor, Jun 2025]** Luma Modify Video (Ray2): takes the live-action video itself plus optional first frame; adhere/flex/reimagine presets; described as using pose, facial expression and scene structure. Third-party docs indicate the same preset names persist on a newer Ray 3.2 edit model. — [Luma: Introducing Modify Video](https://lumalabs.ai/news/introducing-modify-video); [Scenario docs: Luma](https://docs.scenario.com/get-started/generation/video-generation/video-generation-luma-labs)
- **[Practitioner]** Runway Act-Two: driving performance video + character reference; third-party guides say best results need visible hands and face and that "mismatched character anatomy can reduce transfer quality"; Aleph is positioned as footage editing rather than motion transfer. — [Fuser docs: Runway Act-Two](https://docs.fuser.studio/docs/nodes/video/runway-act-two); [Fuser docs: Runway Aleph](https://docs.fuser.studio/docs/nodes/video/runway-aleph)

### Inferences
- "Understands zero-shot" is established for Seedance + depth only by practitioner reports and by the studio's own experience; for skeleton-on-black, Canny, normals, flow visualisations and composites there is no public test as of 2026-11. These need a first-party A/B on a fixed clip.
- DreamActor-M2 demonstrates that the Seedance architecture family consumes motion references as in-context video tokens. If Seedance 2.x's omni-reference works similarly (not confirmed anywhere), the model has no modality-specific pathway for "depth" or "pose": it interprets whatever pixels it is given as a video whose motion/camera should be imitated. That would explain both why depth works without being announced as a feature and why it copies the silhouette — and suggests that any representation that still *looks like a coherent moving scene* (e.g. a proxy-mannequin depth render) is more likely to be read correctly than abstract encodings (flow colour wheels, Plücker maps).
- Because Seedance exposes no documented strength control, the only levers are the reference's information content (representation, resolution, length), prompt wording and how many/which image references are supplied.
- Real-face filtering on reference videos is an additional practical reason to prefer abstracted references (depth/skeleton/proxy) over degraded RGB.

### Gaps
- No official ByteDance statement on which control-map types Seedance 2.x was trained on; the second half of the BytePlus page (prompt guide / parameter tables beyond 100k chars) was not read, so a strength-like parameter there cannot be ruled out. A third-party Chinese guide mentions adjusting "影响强度" (influence strength), but that appears to be a reseller-platform feature and was not verified — [aireiter guide](https://aireiter.com/zh/blog/seedance-2-reference-video-guide).
- One third-party guide surfaced in search (not opened; likely the Atlas Cloud omni-reference guide) says a strong motion reference can override product identity and suggests weighting image references more; unverified — [Atlas Cloud guide](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/case-studies/generative-ai-model-seedance-2-0-a-guide-to-all-round-reference).
- Whether a first-frame anchor can be combined with a `reference_video` in Seedance 2.5: the official page (portion read) is silent, the clipdance article says first-frame and omni-reference inputs cannot be mixed (written about 2.0, June 2026). The studio's own pipeline evidently does combine them, so this source may be outdated or platform-specific.
- No Korean-language practitioner test was found; Chinese results were general reviews (motion and camera replication praised) without depth/skeleton/white-model comparisons — e.g. [302.AI benchmark review](https://302.ai/blog/302-ai-benchmark-lab-review-on-seedance-2-0/).
- Kling O1/2.6, Runway and Luma: no tests found of feeding them depth or skeleton videos as plain references.
- Official Runway Act-Two / Aleph documentation was not retrieved; statements above are from third-party integrator docs.

## Q4. Practical tricks to reduce silhouette lock-in while keeping camera work

### Takeaway
Documented tricks fall into four families: (1) lower the *spatial* information of the reference (downscale, blur) — cheap, but trades away detail unless the model was trained with that knob; (2) destroy the outline specifically (blocky/dilated "shape-agnostic" regions, bone-scale randomisation) while leaving the rest intact; (3) split foreground and background so camera/scene come from one signal and the character from another; (4) control-strength / schedule / prompt levers — most of which exist in open stacks (VACE) but not in Seedance's documented API.

### Cited Findings
- **Downscale the reference** — **[Practitioner, Sep 2026]** ReShot (MiniMax H3): `max_side` 320, "A small one carries the moves without the shape"; lower it further if the face drifts toward the reference. — [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)
- **Blur + downsample depth as a graded knob** — **[Paper, Feb 2023]** Gen-1's t_s (0–7 iterations of blur+downsample), chosen over noise for stability; requires training with the level as a conditioning input. — [Gen-1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2302.03011)
- **Blocky / dilated silhouette ("blobbing")** — **[Paper, Feb 2025]** Animate Anyone 2's shape-agnostic mask: max-pool the character region over a (h/10 × w/10) patch grid within its bounding box plus random scale jitter, explicitly to break mask-outline correspondence; compared qualitatively against precise masks (shape leakage) and plain bounding boxes (deformation/fusion artefacts). No quantitative ablation. — [Animate Anyone 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06145v1)
- **Keep depth only where the skeleton is** — **[Paper, Feb 2025]** Animate Anyone 2 masks depth by the skeleton so limb ordering survives but the body outline does not. — [Animate Anyone 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06145v1)
- **Separate foreground and background signals** — **[Paper]** Wan-Animate replacement mode: subject segmented and zeroed out to form an environment video; character driven separately by skeleton + face. Uni3C: camera from re-rendered first-frame point cloud, human from SMPL-X aligned into that world. Time-to-Move: strong adherence only inside motion masks, weak elsewhere ("dual-clock"). — [Wan-Animate](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14055); [Uni3C](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14899); [Time-to-Move](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.08633v1)
- **Mask grow/shrink in open stacks** — **[Practitioner]** Wan2GP VACE: "Use Expand / Shrink Mask when the replacement subject needs more or less room"; "Area processed" can be whole video, masked or non-masked area, so depth can be applied to the background only. — [Wan2GP VACE.md](https://github.com/deepbeepmeep/Wan2GP/blob/main/docs/VACE.md)
- **Re-proportion the signal instead of weakening it** — **[Paper]** bone-length retargeting on T-pose (Wan-Animate), SMPL β swap (Champ), TPS warp of the reference object into the target's shape (MotionShot), camera-only optimisation of a 3D skeleton (SCAIL). — [Wan-Animate](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14055); [Champ](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14781); [MotionShot](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.16310v1); [SCAIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05905v3)
- **Compensate lost detail with text** — **[Paper, Jan 2026]** DreamActor-M2: after shape-destroying pose augmentation, an MLLM-written description of the motion restores fine semantics (removing it drops motion rating 4.18 → 3.85). **[Practitioner, 2026]** ReShot (H3): narrating what happens in the grey clip makes the model read it "far better"; state that every action/position/timing/camera move transfers but "its grey depth look is not transferred"; whatever the prompt leaves open, the model fills in. — [DreamActor-M2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21716v1); [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)
- **Prompt wording on Seedance** — **[Practitioner, 2026]** OSideMedia: "Describe the look in your prompt, not the motion"; one identity per body in the reference. DEV guide: let the reference lock camera and action timing and avoid re-describing the motion; reference clips of 3–8 s. **[Vendor]** BytePlus example phrasing: "referring to the motion and camera movement of @Video2". — [OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter); [DEV Community guide](https://dev.to/preecha/how-to-use-reference-video-in-seedance-20-copy-motion-and-camera-moves-4g36); [BytePlus Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- **Shorten / simplify the reference** — **[Practitioner, 2026]** 3–8 s, single clear motion signal, no hard cuts inside the reference, no dead first 0.75 s; "Everything in the clip gets copied, including the boring part at the end." — [OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter); [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)
- **Control strength and schedule (open models only)** — **[Practitioner]** VACE: control strength default 1; start/end percent decide "how tightly it adheres to your control input over time"; relaxing Canny thresholds loosens edges. ReShot recommends strength near 1.0 "to follow the blocking closely" and calls pose the "tighter leash" for dance/fights. — [Comfy hub: Wan2.1 VACE Control Video](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/wan2-1-vace-control-video-2652985596d8/); [RunComfy Wan 2.2 VACE](https://www.runcomfy.com/comfyui-workflows/wan-2-2-vace-in-comfyui-pose-driven-motion-video-workflow); [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot)
- **Drop sub-signals rather than everything** — **[Practitioner, Sep–Nov 2025]** Wan Animate users disable body, face or hand skeletons individually; dropping the face skeleton is suggested for cartoon targets. — [kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper #1262](https://github.com/kijai/ComfyUI-WanVideoWrapper/issues/1262)
- **Product-level looseness presets** — **[Vendor]** Luma Modify adhere → flex → reimagine; Kling "Orientation Matches Image" detaches camera from the reference. — [Luma Modify Video docs](https://docs.lumalabs.ai/docs/modify-video); [Kling Motion Control user guide](https://kling.ai/quickstart/motion-control-user-guide)
- **Depth-specific hygiene** — **[Practitioner, 2026]** use a temporally consistent video-depth model (Video Depth Anything) and pass frames in one batch (chunking causes brightness seams); `clip_percent` 0.5–1 when one very near object darkens the rest; shoot with fast shutter/good light because depth flickers on motion blur. — [ComfyUI-ReShot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/ComfyUI-ReShot); [Comfy hub workflow](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/depth-motion-capture-with-seedance-2-0-085a4bba12f8/)

### Inferences
- Uniform blur is the wrong tool for the studio's problem: it removes camera/parallax cues from the *background* at the same rate as it removes the actor's outline. The paper-backed alternative is *selective* degradation — leave background depth sharp (camera, layout), and degrade or replace only the actor region (Animate Anyone 2's patch-max mask; Time-to-Move's two adherence levels; Wan-Animate's FG/BG split).
- Concrete actor-region treatments suggested by the evidence, cheapest first: (a) segment the actor and apply patch-max/dilation + heavy blur to the depth inside that region only ("depth blob" that keeps position, scale and gross motion but no outline); (b) inpaint the actor out of the depth (background-only depth) and overlay a thick-limbed skeleton retargeted to the character's bone lengths, drawn with depth-like shading so it still reads as a body in space; (c) replace the actor with a re-proportioned 3D proxy (capsules/spheres built to the character's model sheet, driven by estimated 3D pose) rendered to depth with the solved camera — the Champ/SCAIL idea implemented outside the model. Option (c) keeps the modality Seedance is already known to follow (depth clay model) while changing the geometry to the 2D character's proportions.
- Quantising/posterising depth removes fine relief but keeps the outline (the silhouette is a depth discontinuity, which quantisation preserves), so it should reduce "relief" leakage (musculature, clothing folds) but not proportion lock-in. No source tested this.
- Downscale-then-upscale is the practical way to apply the ReShot "small reference" trick to Seedance, since Seedance enforces a minimum reference pixel count (≥ 407,696 px per ReShot's spec note) — the information content is what matters, not the container resolution. Untested.
- Because the anchor image fixes frame 1, mismatch between the anchor's character proportions and the reference's frame-1 silhouette is itself a source of conflict; aligning the proxy/skeleton to the anchor image (Wan-Animate's anchor translation; Kling's "match framing and proportions") should reduce early-frame morphing.
- Text is the documented compensator for whatever the degraded signal no longer says (DreamActor-M2). After blobbing/retargeting, hand shapes, finger gestures and prop handling should be described in the prompt.

### Gaps
- No controlled practitioner test of silhouette dilation/blobbing, FG/BG split depth, quantised depth, or skeleton-over-depth composites on Seedance (or any closed omni-reference model) was found; all of these are extrapolations from trained-model papers.
- No Reddit/Civitai thread specifically about blurring depth to loosen body shape in VACE was retrievable through search; the Nathan Shipley VACE-with-Wan-2.2 Notion guide could not be read (page returned no content) — [guide link](https://nathanshipley.notion.site/Guide-VACE-with-Wan-2-2-in-ComfyUI-27191e115364802186aacc04c00d71f3).
- No evidence on whether supplying multiple image references (character sheet + anchor) measurably counteracts silhouette lock-in from a depth reference.
- Whether Seedance tolerates a reference video that mixes modalities (sharp depth background + skeleton/proxy foreground) without rendering the skeleton literally is unknown; MiniMax H3 needed explicit prompt text to avoid copying the grey look.

## Q5. Is there a recommended ranking for "keep camera work + body performance, drop performer shape and identity"?

### Takeaway
No source publishes a ranking for this exact goal; the ordering below is this researcher's synthesis. The evidence supports a clear structural conclusion: no *single* off-the-shelf map does it — depth keeps camera but leaks shape, skeleton drops shape but loses camera — so the best candidates are **hybrids that take camera/scene from (actor-removed or actor-degraded) depth and the performance from a re-proportioned, outline-free character signal**.

### Cited Findings
- Only depth-class signals carry camera, parallax, props and layout in a plain reference video; skeletons do not. — [Comfy hub workflow](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/depth-motion-capture-with-seedance-2-0-085a4bba12f8/); [OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter); [One-to-All Animation](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.22940v2)
- Depth reveals silhouettes and blocks large shape changes. — [Gen-1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2302.03011)
- Skeleton preferred over SMPL because SMPL renders carry shape; SMPL "severe identity leakage". — [Wan-Animate](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.14055); [SCAIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05905v3)
- Even skeletons leak proportions unless retargeted/augmented; retargeting + shape-agnostic regions are the published mitigations. — [EMOSH](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28026); [DreamActor-M2](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.21716v1); [Animate-X](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.10306v2); [Animate Anyone 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06145v1)
- 3D-consistent skeleton proxies (cylinders) beat 2D skeletons on occlusion/depth ambiguity in a trained model (FVD 228.62 vs 295.36). — [SCAIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.05905v3)
- Hybrid skeleton + skeleton-masked depth beats skeleton alone (FVD 186.17 vs 211.15); SMPL-derived depth/normal/semantic + skeleton beats skeleton alone (FVD 170.20 vs 192.34). — [Animate Anyone 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.06145v1); [Champ](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.14781)
- Unified camera + human control in research uses exactly a two-signal split (scene point-cloud render + body model). — [Uni3C](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14899)
- Edges are constrained by source structure and texture. — [Gen-1](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2302.03011); [MotionShot](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.16310v1)
- Seedance has documented support only for "motion and camera movement" of a reference video, practitioner-verified for depth; no documented strength knob. — [BytePlus Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md); [OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)

### Inferences
Proposed ranking for the stated goal on a closed omni-reference model (synthesis; **not** taken from any single source; items 1–3 are untested on Seedance and need A/B validation):

1. **Depth render of a re-proportioned 3D proxy composited into the original scene depth** (actor inpainted out of the depth plate; capsule/sphere mannequin built to the 2D character's proportions, driven by estimated 3D pose, rendered to depth with the solved camera). Keeps: camera, layout, body performance, occlusion order, approximate contact. Drops: actor outline, clothing, face. Rationale: stays inside the one modality Seedance is shown to follow; implements Champ's shape-swap / SCAIL's cylinder proxy / Uni3C's scene+body split outside the model. Costs: 3D pose + camera solve, contact errors after retargeting, hands/face need separate treatment (thick proxy fingers or prompt text).
2. **Background-only (or actor-blobbed) depth + overlaid retargeted skeleton** (bone lengths rescaled to the character on a T-pose, feet-anchored; hands on, face landmarks off). Keeps camera/scene from depth and limb performance from the skeleton. Risks: mixed-modality reference may be rendered literally or ignored; 2D rescaling foreshortening errors; occlusion ambiguity.
3. **Selective degradation of plain depth**: sharp background depth, actor region patch-max dilated / blurred / downscaled ("blob"). Cheapest change from the current pipeline; removes outline but keeps position, scale and gross motion; loses limb articulation and hand detail, which must be supplied by prompt. Expected to help silhouette lock-in less than 1–2 because overall body *extent* (height, width) still leaks.
4. **Plain video depth (current baseline)**, optionally at low resolution. Best camera fidelity; documented silhouette/proportion leak.
5. **Retargeted skeleton alone on black**. Lowest shape leak among simple signals, but no camera/scene; camera would have to come from prompt text or a second reference video — a combination with no public test.
6. **Degraded RGB (blur/pixelate/grayscale)**. Carries everything coarsely; leaks identity/clothing in proportion to remaining detail and may trip real-face filters; only models trained for raw-RGB driving (DreamActor-M2 end-to-end, Kling Motion Control, Luma Modify, Runway Act-Two) are documented to separate motion from appearance.
7. **SMPL mesh / DensePose / normal renders of the actor (un-retargeted)**. Shape leak at least as bad as depth with no camera information.
8. **Canny / HED / lineart**. Leaks outline plus texture and clothing detail; worst for this goal.

Not rankable for Seedance: optical-flow visualisations, point-track videos, Plücker/camera-pose inputs and attention-guidance methods — these have the best theoretical shape-freedom but require models trained or opened up for them (VACE flow control, Go-with-the-Flow, Motion Prompting, Time-to-Move, DiTFlow); no evidence a closed general model reads them as a plain reference video.

- If the studio can move part of the pipeline to an open stack, the literature-preferred route for "performance without shape" is a dedicated animation model with retargeting (Wan-Animate with pose retargeting, SCAIL, One-to-All), but those inherit the skeleton's lack of camera control (author-stated for One-to-All) — so the camera would still need a separate mechanism (e.g. Uni3C-style point-cloud render or VACE depth on the background).

### Gaps
- No published head-to-head for this goal on any model; the ranking is untested on Seedance 2.5 and should be validated with one fixed clip containing a camera move, a turn, a hand gesture and a prop, scored separately for camera fidelity, pose fidelity and proportion preservation of the 2D design.
- No evidence on how Seedance resolves conflicts between a first-frame anchor and a reference whose first-frame silhouette differs strongly (the central risk for options 1–3).
- Cost/robustness of monocular 3D pose + camera solving for option 1 on real production footage was not researched (out of the assigned scope).
