# Seedance 2.x reference-video behaviour and control-video types (skeleton / depth / canny / greybox) — research notes

Research date: 2026-11-15. Labels used on every finding: **[OFFICIAL]** = ByteDance / Volcengine / BytePlus first-party page or paper; **[HOST-DOC]** = third-party API host or reseller documentation (Runware, apiyi, fal, ComfyUI, Atlas Cloud etc. — not ByteDance, but usually derived from the upstream API); **[COMMUNITY]** = hands-on report by a tool author / creator; **[MARKETING]** = vendor blog with unverified claims. Version tags: **[1.x] [2.0] [2.5]**.

Reading caveat: most pages were read through a fetch-and-summarise tool, so "the page does not mention X" means "the summariser found no mention of X in the part it read". The BytePlus 2.5 doc is ~185k characters and was read in two passes; the Volcengine (Chinese) Ark doc itself was never reached — the BytePlus ModelArk doc (overseas twin) is used as the official API source.

## Q1. What do the official docs say about reference video input in Seedance 2.0 / 2.5?

### Takeaway
Officially, Seedance 2.0 takes up to 3 reference videos (9 images, 3 audio; ~15 s total video) and Seedance 2.5 (released 2026-07-31) takes up to 10 reference videos (30 images, 10 audio; 2–30 s each, 30 s total, 24–60 fps, ≥ ~408k pixels), addressed as `@Video1` / `@视频1` in the prompt; the model itself decides from prompt wording whether a job is "reference-to-video", "edit" or "extend". Official text describes reference videos only in semantic terms (motion, camera work, framing, "cinematic language") — real-human faces in reference images/videos are explicitly not accepted for direct upload.

### Cited Findings

**Seedance 2.0 (launched Feb 2026; paper Apr 2026)**
- [OFFICIAL][2.0] The Seedance 2.0 paper (arXiv 2604.14148, submitted 2026-04-15, "Team Seedance") describes a native multimodal audio-video model with four input modalities (text, image, audio, video), 4–15 s output at 480p/720p, and states the platform "supports up to 3 video clips, 9 images, and 3 audio clips". The summariser found no occurrence of pose / skeleton / depth / control-signal terminology in the abstract page. — [arXiv 2604.14148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14148)
- [OFFICIAL][2.0] ByteDance Seed's launch post confirms four-modality input and the 3 video / 9 image / 3 audio open-platform limit. — [seed.bytedance.com Seedance 2.0 launch](https://seed.bytedance.com/en/blog/official-launch-of-seedance-2-0)
- [HOST-DOC][2.0] ComfyUI's built-in ByteDance node docs: each reference video must be ≥ 1.8 s, total reference video duration ≤ 15.1 s, max 3 reference videos; 2.0 / 2.0 Fast / 2.0 Mini accept 9 images, 3 videos, 3 audio, 9 assets, while 2.5 accepts 30 images, 10 videos, 10 audio, 30 assets. — [docs.comfy.org ByteDance2ReferenceNode](https://docs.comfy.org/zh/built-in-nodes/ByteDance2ReferenceNode)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0] Chinese tutorials for Jimeng "全能参考" (omni reference) describe the 2.0 limits as "视频最多3个(总时长≤15秒),用于参考运镜、动作节奏" and the `@` syntax such as "@图1为首帧,参考@视频1的打斗动作". — [Tencent News guide, 2026-02-13](https://news.qq.com/rain/a/20260213A02AB700); [Zhihu tutorial](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2004523300588643639)

**Seedance 2.5 (released 2026-07-31)**
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] Seed blog "一镜成片,随心参考|Seedance 2.5 正式发布" (2026-07-31): single generation up to 30 s (was 15 s), multi-round extension; up to 30 images, 10 video clips, 10 audio clips per submission; upgraded "白模参考 / 动作参考 / 创意参考" (greybox, motion and creative reference); strengthened "绿幕编辑、视角编辑、参考编辑". Rolled out first on 即梦 (Jimeng web) and 豆包专业版; Volcano Ark API "近期" (soon) at time of post. — [seed.bytedance.com Seedance 2.5 launch (zh)](https://seed.bytedance.com/zh/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] Same post, greybox: "在白模参考中，用户可以用无纹理 3D 模型搭出画面的空间结构、主体姿态、运动轨迹和镜头机位" — i.e. an untextured 3D model defines spatial structure, subject pose, motion trajectory and camera position; the model also reads lighting direction/colour temperature/shadows from the greybox. Example edit prompt: "编辑 @视频 1，保持人物、动作及画风不变，仅调整运镜。" — [seed.bytedance.com Seedance 2.5 launch (zh)](https://seed.bytedance.com/zh/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] Seedance 2.5 project page: the model "Understands reference videos more precisely -- capturing the intention, framing, and cinematic language", going "beyond motion transfer into creative interpretation"; "Provides advanced capabilities such as white-model control and green-screen editing"; 30 s per generation, extendable twice. No skeleton/pose/depth/lineart terms on the page. — [seed.bytedance.com/seedance2_5](https://seed.bytedance.com/seedance2_5)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] BytePlus ModelArk doc, model IDs `dreamina-seedance-2-5-260628` and `dreamina-seedance-2-5-flash-261115`. Omni reference: 2.5 = 30 images + 10 videos + 10 audio (50 total); 2.5 flash = 10 + 5 + 5. Total reference audio/video duration cap "increases from 15 seconds to 30 seconds". Output 480p / 720p (8-bit) / 1080p (10-bit) for 2.5 (flash: 480p/720p); ratios 21:9, 16:9, 4:3, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16, adaptive; duration 4–30 s or -1 (auto); mp4 / mov output. — [docs.byteplus.com ModelArk Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] BytePlus reference-video input requirements: mp4/mov (H.264 or H.265); width and height each 300–6000 px; total pixels between 407,696 (e.g. 614×664) and 8,295,044; aspect ratio (w/h) 0.4–2.5; 24–60 fps; < 200 MB per video; 2–30 s per video for non-editing tasks (4–30 s for edit); all videos combined ≤ 30 s. Reference images: 300–6000 px per side, ratio 0.4–2.5, < 30 MB. — [docs.byteplus.com ModelArk Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] Addressing: assets go in the `content` array with `role` = `reference_image` / `reference_video` / `reference_audio` (or `first_frame` / `last_frame`); prompts use `@Video 1`, `@Image1` or plain "refer to Image 1". The doc's own example phrase is "referring to the motion and camera movement of @Video2" — prompt wording, not a separate parameter. — [docs.byteplus.com ModelArk Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] Task type is inferred from assets + prompt intent; `omni_reference_task_type` can be set (BytePlus lists `auto`, `edit`, `extend`, `reference`). Edit requires `ratio=adaptive`, `duration=-1`; extend requires `ratio=adaptive`; first-frame / first-last-frame roles also require `ratio=adaptive`. Violations return `InvalidParameter.TaskTypeConstraint`; declared-vs-detected mismatch returns `InvalidParameter.TaskTypeMismatch`. Edit trigger words include "edit the video", "add", "delete/remove", "modify/replace/change". — [docs.byteplus.com ModelArk Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- [HOST-DOC][2.5] apiyi's FAQ (model `doubao-seedance-2-5-260628`) says there is "没有单独的「参考生视频」开关" and lists only `auto` / `edit` / `extend` for `omni_reference_task_type` — **this conflicts with the BytePlus page, which lists a `reference` value** (possibly a later addition or a mainland/overseas difference). apiyi also warns that wording such as "保持…不变 / 保留", "高清化、修复、增强、去噪" can get a job classified as *edit* even though not on the keyword list, and recommends "参考、借鉴、模仿" plus naming what is referenced (动作 / 运镜 / 风格 / 人物形象). — [docs.apiyi.com seedance2-reference-vs-edit](https://docs.apiyi.com/faq/seedance2-reference-vs-edit)
- [HOST-DOC][2.5] Billing: jobs with a reference video are charged for input-video frames (longer / higher-resolution reference = more tokens). — [docs.apiyi.com seedance2-reference-vs-edit](https://docs.apiyi.com/faq/seedance2-reference-vs-edit)
- [HOST-DOC][2.5] Runware's guide: `inputs.referenceVideos` up to 10 with combined duration "under 30 seconds"; the model takes camera movement (orbit, dolly, pan, handheld drift), pacing, framing, motion trajectory and lip-sync duration from the video; "a motion signal, not the frame content … Seedance rebuilds the shot around the movement". — [Runware Seedance 2.5 motion-control guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/bytedance-seedance-2-5/guides/motion-control.md)

**Real-person restriction**
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] BytePlus: "Seedance 2.5 series does not support directly uploading reference images or videos containing real human faces." Sanctioned alternatives: trusted model outputs from the same account, preset digital characters, authorised real-person assets. — [docs.byteplus.com ModelArk Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- [HOST-DOC][2.0] apiyi: 2.0 "cannot accept reference images with realistic human faces directly" (HTTP 400, not billed); real people need a liveness scan by the subject ("no proxy is allowed") yielding an `asset://` ID; AI-generated realistic humans go through automatic "virtual avatar" ingest; stylised/anime characters need no ingest. — [docs.apiyi.com seedance2-asset-face-reference](https://docs.apiyi.com/en/faq/seedance2-asset-face-reference)
- [HOST-DOC][2.0] Adobe's community forum thread title states reference content "can't include real human faces when using Seedance 2.0 and Seedance 2.0 Fast" (Firefly-hosted route). — [Adobe community thread](https://community.adobe.com/questions-404/reference-content-can-t-include-real-human-faces-when-using-seedance-2-0-and-seedance-2-0-fast-1631867)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0] Early-2026 Chinese coverage reported Jimeng urgently suspended real-person images/videos as subject reference after the launch. — [Sina news](https://www.sina.cn/news/detail/5264146682876558.html)

### Inferences
- The studio's 21:9 depth video is inside the official envelope (ratio 2.33 < 2.5) but must be ≥ ~408k pixels (e.g. ≥ 980×420) and 24–60 fps on the official 2.5 API; a very small "low-res" control video (e.g. 320 px long side, as one community tool recommends for another model) would violate the BytePlus minimum pixel count unless a host auto-upscales it.
- Because task type is inferred from wording, prompts for motion transfer should use "refer to / follow the motion and camera of @Video1" and avoid "keep … unchanged", "replace", "edit", "enhance" — otherwise the job may be reclassified as *video editing* (which preserves original pixels and forces adaptive ratio / source duration), a different behaviour from motion reference.
- A depth or skeleton video contains no real human face, so it side-steps the face-upload restriction; this is consistent with why the depth workflow is popular, but ByteDance does not say so.

### Gaps
- The Volcengine Ark Chinese doc page (volcengine.com/docs) was not reached; all API-level "official" facts come from the BytePlus ModelArk English doc. Mainland and overseas limits may differ (e.g. the `reference` task-type value).
- No official statement was found on *what representation* the model extracts from reference videos beyond semantic descriptions; no official weighting / strength parameter for reference videos was found.
- Jimeng / Dreamina help-centre pages (jimeng.jianying.com, dreamina.capcut.com) were not fetched; UI-level limits (e.g. whether Jimeng's 2.5 UI exposes all 10 video slots) are unverified.
- Seedance 1.x: no source consulted in this run describes reference-video input for 1.0 / 1.5; not verified here (the reference-video / omni-reference feature is consistently described as new in 2.0).
- Exact Seedance 2.0 per-video limits on the official API (fps, pixel range) were not retrieved (the BytePlus page fetched for 2.0 turned out to be an asset-library page).

## Q2. Is there any official or semi-official statement that Seedance understands pose skeletons, depth videos, edge/lineart videos or other ControlNet-style condition videos? Is there a dedicated motion-control / 动作模仿 mode?

### Takeaway
No. As of 2026-11-15 nothing official (Seed blog, project page, 2.0 paper, BytePlus API doc) mentions skeleton/OpenPose/DWPose, depth maps, canny/lineart, normal maps, segmentation or optical-flow as supported condition inputs, and there is no dedicated pose-control parameter or "motion control" endpoint — motion transfer is done by the general omni-reference mechanism plus prompt wording. The only abstract control input that ByteDance officially names is the **white-model / greybox (白模) reference** in Seedance 2.5 (plus green-screen editing).

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] The only officially named structural control is 白模参考: untextured 3D model → "空间结构、主体姿态、运动轨迹和镜头机位"; "动作参考" (motion reference) is listed as upgraded but with no technical detail. No skeleton / depth / lineart / storyboard wording. — [Seed blog, 2026-07-31](https://seed.bytedance.com/zh/blog/one-take-creation-flexible-referencing-introducing-seedance-2-5)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] Project page lists "white-model control and green-screen editing" as advanced capabilities and frames reference-video understanding as going "beyond motion transfer into creative interpretation". — [seed.bytedance.com/seedance2_5](https://seed.bytedance.com/seedance2_5)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] BytePlus API doc: no white-model, skeleton, pose, depth or lineart parameter; no dedicated motion-reference feature — only prompt phrasing like "referring to the motion and camera movement of @Video2". — [docs.byteplus.com ModelArk Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- [OFFICIAL][2.0] The 2.0 paper's arXiv page mentions "multi-modal content reference and editing capabilities" but no pose/skeleton/depth/control-signal conditioning. — [arXiv 2604.14148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14148)
- [HOST-DOC][2.5] Runware's "motion control" guide is just omni-reference usage (reference video = motion, reference image = identity); it documents a "grey untextured 3D clay pre-visualisation render" / "gray clay turntable" → finished shot use case ("Follow the exact turntable motion and camera path") and does not mention skeleton, pose, depth or control maps. — [Runware motion-control guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/bytedance-seedance-2-5/guides/motion-control.md)
- [HOST-DOC][2.0→2.5] ComfyUI's official hub hosts a "Depth Motion Capture with Seedance 2.0" workflow (author: Purz) that feeds a Depth Anything V2 video into the Seedance reference-video input — semi-official endorsement by a host (Comfy Org), not by ByteDance. It explicitly contrasts itself with pose: "Pose transfer uses 2D keypoints; this workflow uses per-frame depth maps." — [comfy.org Depth Motion Capture workflow](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/depth-motion-capture-with-seedance-2-0-085a4bba12f8/)
- [MARKETING][2.5] Atlas Cloud blog (Kishi, 2026-08-07) says "green screen films or white model (geometric blockout)" videos can be uploaded as "Spatial R2V References" to define camera trajectories, depth boundaries and multi-character paths; no pose/skeleton mention; claims are unverified vendor copy. — [Atlas Cloud blog](https://www.atlascloud.ai/blog/guides/seedance-2-5-ai-video-generator-character-consistency)
- [COMMUNITY/MARKETING][2.5] Chinese coverage of the 2.5 greybox workflow: a Zhihu "invited beta" post claims an official Blender plug-in exports greybox plus camera-track files straight into Jimeng; a Bilibili video shows a Blender greybox previs → Seedance 2.5 pipeline; zuoshipin's test (posted 8/1) says the greybox "提供了奔跑路径、建筑结构和相机运动" and the model added materials/characters/atmosphere, while noting plug-in integration is conditional ("如果 Maya、Blender 插件能够稳定传递相机与白模信息"). — [Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2056479504332231493); [Bilibili BV1x7Yx6YEM6](https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1x7Yx6YEM6/); [zuoshipin](https://www.zuoshipin.com/article/18305)
- [COMMUNITY — unreliable] Some CSDN "bbs" articles describe Seedance 2.0 as extracting joint coordinates / joint angular-velocity curves ("动作标尺系统"); the search layer itself flagged these as inconsistent with all mainstream descriptions; they look auto-generated and should not be cited as evidence of skeleton support. — [CSDN bbs article](https://bbs.csdn.net/weixin_33664951/article/details/100154991)

### Inferences
- Seedance treats every reference video as *a video to be understood semantically*, not as a pixel-aligned ControlNet condition. Depth/skeleton/canny videos work only to the extent that the model can "read" motion, layout and camera from them the same way it would from any footage. This explains both the benefit (appearance removed) and the two problems the studio sees (flicker in the control video is "seen" as motion; a body-shaped depth blob is "seen" as the body shape).
- The officially supported 白模 input is conceptually the closest sanctioned thing to the studio's depth "clay model": a flat-shaded grey 3D-looking render. A depth map resembles a greybox render with no lighting; a normal-map-lit or matcap-style grey render would resemble the official 白模 distribution more closely (speculation — untested in any source found).

### Gaps
- No official statement, positive or negative, on skeleton or depth input was found. "Not documented" ≠ "does not work".
- Whether Jimeng's 2.5 UI has a distinct "动作参考 / 动作模仿" entry (as opposed to omni-reference with wording) was not confirmed; searches for "即梦 动作模仿" returned only 2.0-era omni-reference tutorials.
- The claimed official Blender plug-in (greybox + camera track import) rests on one promotional Zhihu/Sohu post; not confirmed on a ByteDance page.

## Q3. What have users reported about feeding skeleton / depth / greybox / canny videos into Seedance 2.x? Is a stick figure read as motion or rendered literally? Does camera motion survive a black background?

### Takeaway
Depth-map reference video is a widely used, repeatedly reported community technique on Seedance 2.0 and 2.5 (ComfyUI hub workflow, at least two open-source converter tools, X posts, vendor templates), and greybox/3D-previs video is both community-tested and officially supported on 2.5. For **skeleton video specifically I found no documented hands-on Seedance test** — only a tool (ReShot) that generates DWPose skeleton videos "for Seedance 2.0/2.5" and ships a Seedance prompt for it, while its actual skeleton results were produced on a different model (MiniMax H3); the tool authors and a second tool author both state that a skeleton carries no camera or scene information.

### Cited Findings

**Depth video (well attested)**
- [COMMUNITY/HOST][2.0] ComfyUI hub workflow by Purz: Depth Anything V2 (vitl, 512 px, per-frame "Relative") → depth video into Seedance reference-video input + one reference image; prompt in the shipped JSON is just "dance"; 5 s, 720p, adaptive ratio. Claims: "The depth map dictates how things move; the image dictates what they are"; depth "preserves camera motion and scene geometry (parallax, relative distances)". Stated limits: "Depth quality drops with motion blur or low light", flicker/distortion on fast motion; identity and background of the source are not kept "by design". — [comfy.org workflow](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/depth-motion-capture-with-seedance-2-0-085a4bba12f8/)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0] X post by Nexlow titled "The depth map trick that fixed dance accuracy in Seedance 2.0": feeding raw dance footage makes the model interpret person, clothes, lighting and room along with movement; a depth map leaves "nothing left to interpret" but structure (post body paywalled/402 — only the search snippet was read). — [X / NexlowX](https://x.com/NexlowX/status/2077796149617139742?lang=en)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0 + 2.5] OSideMedia `depth-converter` (open-source, dated live tests 2026-07-25 on 2.0 via Ark through 2026-11-11 on 2.5): "Seedance treats a video reference as a motion + camera channel: it reproduces choreography, walk cadence, dolly, orbit and whip-pan camera paths, and timing." "Seedance follows video refs well but not deterministically." "Refs beyond ~10 s and multi-signal clips degrade; fast motion can flicker in the output." "The ref outranks the prompt." — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0 vs 2.5] Same tool, measured: on 2.0, 1080p generations ran +22–32% "hot" on motion and failed to adhere ("1080p was 0 for 3, across three seeds, two reference resolutions and two prompt phrasings") → rule "test at 480p, confirm at 720p … Never 1080p"; on 2.5 "the 0 for 3 does not transfer" (one 1080p fire adhered on 2026-08-24, but "Two passes are not proof"). — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0 vs 2.5] Same tool, start frame: on 2.0 "a start frame silently drops the depth ref"; on 2.5 "you may use it" because "the frame demotes to a reference image and the refs ride." — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- [COMMUNITY] Same tool, depth-ref hygiene: depth ref "defaults to 720p-class or below: it is a motion guide, not picture"; letterbox bars must be cropped ("A depth model reads a flat black matte as a surface"); target a 3–8 s window with one motion signal; a frozen first 0.75 s makes "the generation … sit still and then start"; hard cuts inside the ref are "the worst kind"; experimental variants (contrast stretch, inverted polarity, mid-grey cap, Canny-on-depth "edges") are offered as untested "experiments", "Upload exactly one per generation." — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- [MARKETING][2.5] BudgetPixel's Video Depth Anything page tells users to pick Seedance 2.5, add the depth video as reference video and describe the new scene (examples: dancer; skateboarder → snowboarder). — [budgetpixel.com](https://budgetpixel.com/models/video-depth-anything)

**Skeleton / pose video (thin evidence, none first-hand on Seedance)**
- [COMMUNITY] Maosika's open-source ReShot ("copy the shot, not the actors") converts a clip into depth (Video Depth Anything), pose (DWPose "coloured stick figures on black, hands included, faces not", with tracking, hysteresis and One-Euro smoothing; added in v0.5.0) or canny, explicitly "for Seedance 2.0/2.5 reference video, MiniMax H3 … and Wan VACE", with a `--target seedance` preset. — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- [COMMUNITY — other model] ReShot's reported skeleton results are on **MiniMax H3, not Seedance**: "We re-made the three demo takes on MiniMax H3 with the skeleton as `<Video 1>`"; "All three skeleton takes reproduce the six beats... at the same level as the depth takes"; caveat "One take per condition with different seeds, so read it as 'pose works as a reference video too', not 'pose is better'." (Tool measurements "Verified on rented cards on 2026-09-12".) — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- [COMMUNITY] For Seedance, ReShot only supplies prompt text: "参考@视频1的动作与运镜，顺序与视频保持一致。" and a skeleton variant adding "画面里不要出现骨架线条" (skeleton lines must not appear in frame); its README states the stick-figure look "is not transferred" as the intended outcome, and for depth warns "Say that the grey look is not to be copied, or you may get a grey film back." — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- [COMMUNITY] What a skeleton drops, per ReShot: pose keeps "every limb and hand, exactly" and drops "everything else, including body shape and the set"; camera is carried by depth ("the camera—push-ins, tracking, handheld shake") and canny, not by skeleton content. Recommendation: pose for "dance and martial arts", depth "for everything else"; when unsure generate both (`--control depth,pose`). Pose fails in tight close-ups: "The estimator still guesses where the shoulders and hips are when they are off screen." — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- [COMMUNITY] depth-converter's author makes the same point from the other side: depth "keeps what pose skeletons throw away" (camera path, props, scene geometry), and ships no pose workflow. — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- [COMMUNITY] ComfyUI workflow page: "Pose transfer uses 2D keypoints; this workflow uses per-frame depth maps" — depth chosen because it keeps camera motion and parallax. — [comfy.org workflow](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/depth-motion-capture-with-seedance-2-0-085a4bba12f8/)

**Canny / lineart**
- [COMMUNITY] ReShot canny = "white edge lines on black", keeps "composition and outlines"; README notes canny "carries the outline of clothes and faces too" (i.e. more appearance leakage than depth/pose); use when "you want the picture's layout, not only the people". No Seedance-specific result reported. — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)

**3D greybox / clay previs video (officially supported on 2.5, community-tested)**

- [HOST-DOC][2.5] Runware "clay pre-vis to finished shot": grey clay render supplies motion and pacing, a packshot image supplies materials. — [Runware guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/bytedance-seedance-2-5/guides/motion-control.md)
- [COMMUNITY][2.5] Sohu article: designers build untextured grey models for space/pose/camera, upload greybox screenshots from several angles, and the model renders material and light; claims greybox "locks proportion and structure". — [Sohu](https://www.sohu.com/a/1062058997_122569971)

**Raw live-action reference (baseline)**
- [COMMUNITY][2.0] Zhihu testers: with a raw reference video, "人物动作，分镜和运镜几乎与原视频保持的非常一致"; another notes outputs sometimes drift (actions/lines reproduced but content "有点像别的剧"). — [Zhihu answer (卡尔的AI沃茨)](https://www.zhihu.com/question/2004562605960873343/answer/2005237589863592426); [Zhihu article](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2004220619311556450)

### Inferences
- Skeleton video as a Seedance reference is **plausible but unverified**: evidence = (1) Seedance interprets reference videos semantically; (2) a comparable "omni-reference" model (MiniMax H3) accepted DWPose stick figures as motion reference at parity with depth in a single-take test; (3) a tool vendor felt confident enough to ship a Seedance skeleton preset with a "don't draw the skeleton lines" prompt — which itself implies literal stick-figure rendering is a known risk that must be prompted against.
- With a skeleton-on-black video there is no background parallax, so camera *translation/orbit/handheld* cannot be recovered from it; only apparent framing change of the figure (scale/position) remains, which the model may read as either character motion or zoom. Both tool authors say this explicitly. For the studio's use case (camera work matters), skeleton alone would lose the camera; a hybrid (skeleton composited over a low-detail depth/greybox background, or skeleton + depth as two references) is the logical test — no source tested it.
- Skeleton is the one control type that removes body silhouette (problem b), which is why ReShot lists "body shape" among things pose drops. But the 2D character's proportions would then have to come entirely from the image reference + prompt; multiple sources warn motion transfer degrades when the target body plan differs from the source.
- The studio's flicker (problem a) is exactly what the tools above engineered around: both use **Video Depth Anything** (temporally consistent, overlapping 32-frame windows) instead of per-frame Depth Anything V2, and normalise depth once over the whole clip rather than per frame.

### Gaps
- **No first-hand, documented Seedance 2.0 or 2.5 test of an OpenPose/DWPose skeleton reference video was found** in English, Chinese or Korean searches (Reddit, Bilibili, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, DCInside/Arca Live/Naver returned nothing specific). Whether Seedance renders literal stick figures, and how often, is unanswered by sources.
- No source tested skeleton + depth as two simultaneous reference videos, nor normal-map, segmentation-mask, optical-flow-visualisation, blurred or pixelated videos as Seedance references.
- ReShot's README was read via a summariser; it is possible the repo contains Seedance skeleton demo clips not captured in the summary. Worth a direct read by the studio.
- Korean community sources: none found on this specific question (searches returned only generic Seedance explainers).
- Xiaohongshu / Douyin / WeChat content is poorly indexed by the search tool used; absence there is not evidence of absence.

## Q4. How do prompt wording and reference roles change behaviour?

### Takeaway
Role assignment is done entirely in the prompt: name the video as the source of motion + camera, name the image(s) as the source of identity/design, describe the *look* rather than re-describing the motion, and tell the model the control-video look itself (grey / skeleton lines) must not appear. On 2.5, wording also determines the task type, so "keep/replace/enhance"-style phrasing can flip a motion-reference job into a video-edit job.

### Cited Findings
- [HOST-DOC][2.5] Runware recommended wording: "Transfer the exact movement and timing from @Video1 onto the character in @Image1."; add "preserve every gesture and the exact lip-sync duration of each phrase" to pin pacing; add "Preserve the character's design and proportions throughout."; for clay previs: "Follow the exact turntable motion and camera path," then describe materials and lighting. A video with no identity image "reinvents the face, product, and wardrobe each run". — [Runware guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/bytedance-seedance-2-5/guides/motion-control.md)
- [HOST-DOC][2.5] Runware: match output width/height (or resolution/ratio) to the source's shape — a landscape source with portrait output "pulls the model between two structures". — [Runware guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/bytedance-seedance-2-5/guides/motion-control.md)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0/2.5] depth-converter: "Describe the look in your prompt, not the motion."; "Add one identity per body in the ref." (in a test with one identity against a two-body reference, "the model cloned that identity rather than inventing a stranger"); "The ref outranks the prompt." — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- [COMMUNITY] ReShot's approach (developed on MiniMax H3, offered for Seedance): "Say in words what happens in the grey clip"; "Say that the grey look is not to be copied, or you may get a grey film back."; state relative character size explicitly when the 2D design differs from the performer (bear example: "about 1.3× the rabbit, never more than 1.5×"); Seedance wording "参考@视频1的动作与运镜，顺序与视频保持一致。" (+ "画面里不要出现骨架线条" for skeleton). Also: "Everything in the clip gets copied, including the boring part at the end" and "Whatever the prompt leaves open, the model fills with text and badges." — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- [COMMUNITY] ReShot on size of the control video: "A full-size grey silhouette starts to pull the character's face shape towards the person in the reference; a small one carries the moves without the shape." (recommended `--max-res 320` for H3; note that this author's finding is on H3). — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] BytePlus example phrasing: "referring to the motion and camera movement of @Video2"; edit-type phrasing such as "replace the character in @Video 1 with the character in @Image 1" belongs to the *edit* task (adaptive ratio, source duration). — [docs.byteplus.com ModelArk Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- [HOST-DOC][2.5] apiyi: to stay in reference-to-video (and keep control of ratio/duration), use "参考、借鉴、模仿" and state what is referenced (动作 / 运镜 / 风格 / 人物形象); focus on describing the new shot; avoid "增加、删除、替换、保持不变、高清化、延长". — [docs.apiyi.com](https://docs.apiyi.com/faq/seedance2-reference-vs-edit)
- [OFFICIAL][2.5] `first_frame` role forces `ratio=adaptive`. — [docs.byteplus.com ModelArk Seedance 2.5](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md) — and per community testing, a start frame on 2.5 is demoted to a reference image so the video reference still applies, whereas on 2.0 it silently dropped the depth reference. — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0] Zhihu tutorial: in omni-reference mode each asset's role is assigned with `@` (e.g. reference video for motion rhythm, image as first frame). — [Zhihu](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2004523300588643639)
- [COMMUNITY][2.0] A Zhihu tester reports that using only a video as reference costs about half the credits of video + image references on Jimeng. — [Zhihu article](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2004220619311556450)

### Inferences
- For the studio's silhouette lock-in problem, the documented levers are: (1) an explicit proportions line ("Preserve the character's design and proportions from @Image1 throughout; body proportions come from @Image1, not @Video1"), (2) explicit numeric relative-size statements (ReShot's bear example), (3) a character sheet as an additional image reference so identity has more weight (2.5 allows 30 images), (4) lowering the control video's resolution/detail so it carries motion but less shape (official floor ≈ 408k pixels on 2.5), (5) a control type that has no silhouette (skeleton) — levers 4–5 are untested on Seedance in the sources found.
- "Skeleton + depth as two references with different roles" (e.g. "@Video1 for camera movement and staging, @Video2 for limb motion") is permitted by the API (10 videos, 30 s combined on 2.5; 3 videos, 15 s on 2.0) but note the combined-duration cap halves the usable clip length (two 15 s references on 2.5), and depth-converter reports "multi-signal clips degrade" and "Upload exactly one per generation" for its variants. Untested; flagged as speculative.

### Gaps
- No controlled A/B of prompt wordings on Seedance 2.5 was found (all advice is single-author experience or host documentation).
- No source documents a numeric "reference strength" control in Seedance 2.x; none appears to exist in the BytePlus doc portion read.

## Q5. How do competing models handle the same thing — which explicitly accept skeleton/depth control videos?

### Takeaway
Among the models checked, explicit ControlNet-style condition videos (pose skeleton, depth, canny, MLSD, trajectory) are documented for the open-source **Wan VACE / Wan 2.2 Fun VACE** family; **Kling Motion Control** and **Wan 2.2 Animate** are dedicated motion-transfer modes that take a *raw driving video* of a performer plus a character image (no user-supplied skeleton); Seedance and MiniMax H3 are "semantic omni-reference" models where abstract control videos work only by interpretation. Runway, Luma, Veo and Sora 2 could not be verified in this run.

### Cited Findings
- [HOST-DOC] Wan 2.2 VACE Fun A14B: third-party host pages list supported control conditions as Pose, Depth, MLSD, Canny edges and Trajectory control (aggregator pages, not Alibaba's own card). — [aimlapi.com wan-2-2-vace-fun-a14b-pose](https://aimlapi.com/models/wan-2-2-vace-fun-a14b-pose)
- [HOST-DOC] Wan 2.1 VACE ComfyUI workflow: control video + reference image + prompt; Canny is the default preprocessor and Depth or Lineart preprocessors can be swapped into the same control input. — [comfy.org Wan2.1 VACE Control Video](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/wan2-1-vace-control-video-2652985596d8/)
- [COMMUNITY] One guide states the official Wan 2.2 VACE Fun workflow needs ~60 GB VRAM or more (quantised variants exist). — [stablediffusiontutorials.com](https://www.stablediffusiontutorials.com/2025/09/wan2.2-vace-fun.html)
- [HOST-DOC] Wan 2.2 Animate: two modes — "animate" (image character moves like the subject in the driving video) and "replace" (swap character into the video); driving video should avoid heavy occlusion and strong motion blur. — [wavespeed.ai Wan 2.2 Animate](https://www.wavespeed.ai/models/wavespeed-ai/wan-2.2/animate)
- [OFFICIAL-ish] NVIDIA-hosted model card for "Wan2.2-Animate-2-14B" describes a framework that directly consumes driving videos in a redesigned DiT, avoiding intermediate motion extractors (i.e. no explicit skeleton step). A search snippet dates this variant 2026-08-07; treat the date as unverified. — [build.nvidia.com model card](https://build.nvidia.com/wan-ai/wan2.2-animate-2-14b/modelcard)
- [HOST-DOC] Kling 3.0 Motion Control: two required inputs — character image + reference motion video; reference should contain "a realistic character with visible body, 3–30 seconds" (Fuser docs); duration follows the reference (max 10 s for image orientation, 30 s for video orientation, per a reseller doc); Runway's help page for its hosted Kling 3.0 Motion Control advises "Character proportions should match the reference motion as much as possible-- avoid driving half-body characters with full-body motions". No source mentions skeleton/depth input for Kling. — [docs.fuser.studio](https://docs.fuser.studio/docs/nodes/video/kling-3-0-motion.md); [Runway help: Kling 3.0 Motion Control](https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/50280558448147-Creating-with-Kling-3-0-Motion-Control)
- [OFFICIAL — vendor benchmark] Kling's release page claims large win-rate advantages for VIDEO 3.0 Motion Control over Wan2.2-Animate (Move mode) and Runway Act-Two in Kling's own evaluation (figures as reported in a search snippet; the page body could not be fetched). — [kling.ai model release: motion control](https://kling.ai/dev/model-release/motion-control)
- [COMMUNITY] Korean Threads repost of @genel_ai's comparison (Kling 2.6 era): Kling 2.6 Motion Control reproduces fine finger motion; Runway Act-Two captures the motion but with many errors (pattern distortion, flickering outlines); Wan 2.2 Animate reproduces overall flow but fingertips can deviate. — [Threads @won.wizard](https://www.threads.com/@won.wizard/post/DStrrVkjFJS/video-kling-26-vs-runway-vs-wan-22-motion-control-comparison-by-genel_ai-%ED%98%84%EC%9E%AC%EC%9D%98-%EB%AA%A8%EC%85%98-%EC%BB%A8%ED%8A%B8%EB%A1%A4-%EA%B8%B0%EB%8A%A5)
- [COMMUNITY] MiniMax H3 (another reference-video model) accepted both depth and DWPose skeleton videos as `<Video 1>` motion reference in ReShot's single-take tests; ReShot also targets "MiniMax H3 Fun ControlNet" and Wan VACE. — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- [HOST-DOC] A reseller note says its Wan 2.2 route was retired on 2026-10-24 in favour of Wan 3.0 (availability note only; unverified). — [unifically.com Wan 2.2 Animate](https://unifically.com/blogs/wan-2.2-animate)

### Inferences
- Rough taxonomy for the report writer: (A) *explicit condition-video models* — Wan VACE / Fun-Control family (skeleton, depth, canny documented; pixel-aligned control, self-hosted or via hosts); (B) *dedicated performer-driven motion transfer* — Kling Motion Control, Wan Animate, Runway Act-Two (raw human driving video; designed around a visible realistic body, so a depth blob or stick figure is off-distribution and real-footage input conflicts with the studio's "no identity leak" goal less, since these keep identity from the image); (C) *semantic omni-reference* — Seedance 2.0/2.5, MiniMax H3 (any video is "understood"; abstract control videos are a community hack; greybox is officially blessed on Seedance 2.5).
- If the studio needs guaranteed skeleton adherence without silhouette leakage, category A is the only one where skeleton input is a documented, designed-for feature.

### Gaps
- Runway Act-Two / Aleph, Luma (Modify Video / Ray), Google Veo 3.x and OpenAI Sora 2: no sources were retrieved in this run, so their handling of skeleton/depth control videos is **unverified here**. (Unsourced background from the researcher's prior knowledge, to be re-verified before use: Act-Two is driven by a raw performance video; Aleph is prompt-based video-to-video editing; Luma Modify Video restructures an input video at selectable strength; Veo and Sora 2 have no documented skeleton/depth condition-video input.)
- The original Sept-2025 Wan 2.2 Animate pipeline's use of extracted skeleton + face signals was not source-verified in this run; only the newer "Animate-2" card (no pose extraction) was seen.
- Kling's official Motion Control user guide and Kling O1 / 2.6 reference-video specifics were not successfully fetched (page returned only a heading); all Kling details come from host docs.

## Q6. Known failure modes

### Takeaway
Reported failure modes cluster into: (1) control-video quality being read as motion (flicker, motion blur, letterbox bars, dead starts, hard cuts); (2) shape leakage from depth silhouettes and from mismatched body plans; (3) non-deterministic adherence that worsens with long (>~10 s) or multi-signal references and, on 2.0, at 1080p; (4) control-video look bleeding into output (grey film / skeleton lines) unless prompted against; (5) platform moderation — real faces in references are blocked, and even a faceless depth kit was once refused.

### Cited Findings
- **Flicker / temporal noise:** "Depth quality drops with motion blur or low light"; flicker or distortion on fast motion; keep FPS and aspect ratio consistent; shoot cleaner source ("higher shutter speed, more light"). [2.0] — [comfy.org workflow](https://comfy.org/hub/workflows/depth-motion-capture-with-seedance-2-0-085a4bba12f8/)
- **Per-frame normalisation "breathing":** ReShot normalises depth "once over the whole clip... never per frame" because per-frame normalisation "makes the scene 'breathe'", and uses Video Depth Anything whose overlapping 32-frame windows mean "depth doesn't jitter between frames"; for pose, "A per-frame pose estimator swaps people, jitters joints and blinks limbs at the visibility threshold" (hence tracking + One-Euro filter). — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- **Source quality:** "Motion blur, low light, and shake all degrade the motion signal." [2.5] — [Runware guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/bytedance-seedance-2-5/guides/motion-control.md)
- **Silhouette / body-shape lock-in:** "Depth strips clothing, so every figure reads as a body shape." — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter); "A full-size grey silhouette starts to pull the character's face shape towards the person in the reference; a small one carries the moves without the shape." (observed on MiniMax H3) — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- **Body-plan mismatch:** motion transfer "works best when both share a body plan"; "keep the target's structure close to the source's when the motion has to read exactly." [2.5] — [Runware guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/bytedance-seedance-2-5/guides/motion-control.md)
- **Identity invention / cloning:** video-only reference "reinvents the face, product, and wardrobe each run" — [Runware guide](https://runware.ai/docs/models/bytedance-seedance-2-5/guides/motion-control.md); with fewer identities than bodies in the ref the model "cloned that identity" — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **Canny leaks appearance:** canny "carries the outline of clothes and faces too". — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- **Control look bleeding through:** "Say that the grey look is not to be copied, or you may get a grey film back."; skeleton prompt needs "画面里不要出现骨架线条". — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- **Non-determinism, length, multi-signal, fast motion:** "Seedance follows video refs well but not deterministically."; "Refs beyond ~10 s and multi-signal clips degrade; fast motion can flicker in the output."; a frozen first 0.75 s → output "will sit still and then start"; hard cuts in the ref are the worst spikes; motion parallel to the camera plane has low "depth retention" (weak signal in depth). [2.0/2.5] — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **Output resolution vs adherence:** on 2.0, 1080p output "was 0 for 3" for adherence; on 2.5 this did not reproduce in limited trials. — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **Start frame conflict (2.0):** "a start frame silently drops the depth ref" on 2.0; on 2.5 the frame is demoted to a reference image and the video reference still applies. — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **Skeleton-specific:** pose estimators hallucinate off-screen joints in tight close-ups; skeleton drops body shape, set and camera. — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- **Everything is copied:** "Everything in the clip gets copied, including the boring part at the end."; under-specified prompts get filled "with text and badges". — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot)
- **Task-type misclassification (2.5):** prompts containing keep-unchanged / replace / enhance semantics can be judged as *edit*, returning `InvalidParameter.TaskTypeConstraint` if a fixed ratio/duration was requested, or silently yielding source-length output. — [docs.apiyi.com](https://docs.apiyi.com/faq/seedance2-reference-vs-edit); [docs.byteplus.com](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md)
- **Moderation:** "Seedance 2.5 series does not support directly uploading reference images or videos containing real human faces." — [docs.byteplus.com](https://docs.byteplus.com/docs/en/modelark/seedance-2-5.md); if a clip contains real people "the platform's content check may refuse it outright" — [github.com/maosika-ai/reshot](https://github.com/maosika-ai/reshot); one faceless depth kit ("two depth silhouettes, prone and overlapping, filling the frame") was refused at input, and "Seedance's handling of faces and references may shift, in either direction" — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **Moderation, low-confidence:** a third-party blog claims Seedance 2.0's upload filter runs vision checks on reference videos as well as images, with ~2 s of visible face as a rejection rule of thumb (not from a primary source; page redirected and was not read in full). — [seedance2pro.io blog](https://seedance2pro.io/es/blog/why-is-seedance-2-rejecting-reference-video)
- **Rights:** "Depth strips pixels, not authorship." — converting footage to depth does not remove rights in the underlying performance/choreography. — [github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter](https://github.com/OSideMedia/depth-converter)
- **Hands/fingers (competitor data only):** fine finger fidelity is reported as Kling 2.6 Motion Control's strength and a weak spot for Wan 2.2 Animate and Runway Act-Two. — [Threads @won.wizard](https://www.threads.com/@won.wizard/post/DStrrVkjFJS/video-kling-26-vs-runway-vs-wan-22-motion-control-comparison-by-genel_ai-%ED%98%84%EC%9E%AC%EC%9D%98-%EB%AA%A8%EC%85%98-%EC%BB%A8%ED%8A%B8%EB%A1%A4-%EA%B8%B0%EB%8A%A5)

### Inferences
- Problem (a) "depth shimmer → output wobble" matches the documented cause (per-frame Depth Anything V2 with per-frame normalisation). Source-backed mitigations: switch to Video Depth Anything; normalise over the whole clip; keep the reference ≤ 720p-class; crop letterbox bars; trim to one clean 3–10 s motion beat; avoid dead first frames and cuts.
- Problem (b) "silhouette lock-in" is an expected property of depth ("every figure reads as a body shape"), not a bug in the studio's setup. Source-backed mitigations are lower-detail/smaller control video (H3 evidence only), explicit proportion wording, and one identity image per body; skeleton video is the theoretically cleanest fix for shape but is untested on Seedance and loses camera/scene — so it should be A/B-tested, ideally alongside a hybrid.
- A 3D greybox/mannequin re-render of the performance (e.g. mocap → retargeted onto a proxy with the 2D character's proportions) is the only route that is both *officially supported on 2.5* and removes the performer's proportions at the source; it costs a mocap/retargeting step. (Inference combining the official 白模 statement with the body-plan findings; no source describes this exact pipeline.)

### Gaps
- No Seedance-specific reports on hands/fingers or multi-person behaviour under depth/skeleton control beyond the "one identity per body" note and the single refused two-body kit.
- No quantitative data on how much body-proportion leakage occurs with depth vs skeleton vs greybox on Seedance 2.5.
- The claimed moderation thresholds for reference videos (e.g. "2 seconds of visible face") are unverified.
- A third-party tutorial describing overlaying a grid on faces to get under the face classifier was surfaced in search (useapi.net); it is a filter-evasion technique, was not read, and is not recommended — the sanctioned routes are the asset-authorisation flows above.
